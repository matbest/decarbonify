# Green Business Fund: Capabilities, Features, and Delivery Process (Prompt-Ready)

Use this document as source context for a doc-filling LLM to generate a strong Green Business Fund application and supporting materials.

## 1) Fund Context and Purpose

The Green Business Fund supports Milton Keynes' transition to a low-carbon economy by funding revenue-based projects that help businesses reduce emissions, improve resource efficiency, and build sustainability capability.

The Fund is for organisations that deliver support to businesses, not for improvements to the applicant's own operations.

## 2) Core Fund Rules (Concise)

### Objectives

- Support projects that help multiple businesses across Milton Keynes become more sustainable.
- Contribute to Milton Keynes City Council's net zero ambition for the city by 2030.
- Prioritise interventions with clear, credible, wider business benefits.

### Grant Value

- Up to GBP 25,000.
- No match funding required.
- Revenue expenditure only.

### Geographic Scope

- Activity must support registered businesses within the Milton Keynes administrative area.
- Applicants may be located outside Milton Keynes if delivery and benefits are within Milton Keynes.

### Who Can Apply

Any legally constituted organisation, including:

- Public sector bodies (including town and parish councils)
- Private sector organisations (sole traders, partnerships, limited companies)
- Voluntary, community, and social enterprise organisations

### Deadline and Route

- Submit by 4:00pm on 30 April 2026.
- Submission email: carbon@milton-keynes.gov.uk

### Assessment Timing and Process (Fund Side)

- Eligible applications assessed within approximately 2 weeks after closing.
- Outcomes expected in early May 2026.
- Written feedback available on request.
- If successful in principle, full eligibility checks follow (including subsidy control), then funding agreement.

## 3) Eligible and Ineligible Expenditure

### Eligible (Revenue)

- Professional sustainability services (for example: carbon baselines, reduction pathways, energy/resource audits)
- Software, digital tools, and platforms (for example: licences or tool development/adaptation for SME support)
- Training, workshops, events, certifications, specialist courses, learning materials, digital resources
- Outreach and engagement activity for groups of businesses
- Practical guidance, toolkits, and shared resources for businesses

### Not Eligible

- Activity focused on only one business
- Capital expenditure (for example: solar PV, heat pumps, insulation)
- Improvements to the applicant's own premises or operations
- Costs already funded by another grant/subsidy/source
- Routine business-as-usual overheads not linked to project delivery
- Staff costs not linked to project delivery

## 4) Project Capabilities Relevant to the Fund (Decarbonify-Aligned)

The following capabilities are directly relevant to Green Business Fund objectives and eligible expenditure categories.

### A) Multi-Business Carbon Baseline and Profiling

- Structured onboarding of business assets/activities into a portfolio model
- Rapid baseline emissions estimation at asset, site, and portfolio levels
- Standardised data capture templates to support repeatable delivery across many businesses

### B) Emissions Insight and Hotspot Identification

- Breakdown of emissions by category, asset type, and hierarchy
- Identification of highest-impact emission sources to prioritise interventions
- Clear visual summaries suitable for SME decision-makers

### C) Actionable Recommendation Engine

- Practical decarbonisation recommendations linked to baseline data
- Estimated savings and explainable calculations/assumptions
- Recommendation tracking (for example: ignore/done/status) to support delivery reporting

### D) Resource Efficiency and Operational Improvement Support

- Support for identifying reduction pathways beyond carbon-only framing (energy and resource efficiency)
- Guidance-ready outputs that can be converted into business action plans

### E) Training and Capability-Building Support

- Can be used as a live teaching and facilitation tool in workshops
- Produces understandable outputs suitable for training non-specialist teams
- Supports sustainability capability uplift in groups of businesses

### F) Shared Toolkit and Guidance Production

- Repeatable templates and methods can be packaged as shared guidance/toolkits
- Enables consistent support delivery quality across a cohort of businesses

### G) Evidence and Reporting Readiness

- Structured outputs can support programme reporting on businesses supported and estimated impact
- Consistent methodology improves credibility of aggregate outcomes

## 6) Delivery Process for a Green Business Fund Project

Use this as the operational process narrative in the application.

### Phase 1: Mobilisation and Setup

- Confirm target business cohorts within Milton Keynes
- Finalise project governance, delivery plan, milestones, and risk controls
- Prepare onboarding materials, templates, and communications

### Phase 2: Outreach and Enrolment

- Recruit participating businesses through partner channels and direct outreach
- Confirm eligibility and participation baseline data requirements
- Schedule onboarding clinics/workshops

### Phase 3: Baseline and Diagnostic

- Capture business activity/asset data
- Build portfolio baselines and identify hotspots
- Validate assumptions with participants

### Phase 4: Action Planning and Recommendations

- Generate practical, prioritised recommendations per business
- Provide estimated impact and explainable assumptions
- Define near-term and medium-term actions

### Phase 5: Capability Building and Implementation Support

- Deliver workshops/training sessions and practical guidance
- Support participants to implement no/low-cost and operational changes
- Provide office-hours style support for adoption barriers

### Phase 6: Monitoring, Outcomes, and Reporting

- Track engagement, outputs, and estimated outcomes
- Aggregate impact at cohort/programme level
- Produce end-of-project report and reusable toolkit outputs

## 7) Suggested Outputs and Outcome Measures

Use/adapt these for target-setting and reporting.

### Output Metrics (Delivery Scale)

- Number of businesses engaged
- Number of businesses receiving baseline assessments
- Number of action plans/recommendation packs issued
- Number of workshops delivered
- Number of learners/attendees trained
- Number of toolkits/guidance resources produced

### Outcome Metrics (Impact)

- Estimated annual tCO2e reduction identified
- Estimated resource efficiency improvements identified
- Number/percentage of businesses implementing at least one action
- Confidence/capability uplift scores (pre/post support)

### Value for Money Indicators

- Cost per business supported
- Cost per baseline delivered
- Cost per estimated tCO2e identified for reduction
- Evidence of scalable and reusable delivery assets

## 8) Assessment-Criteria Alignment (Write-Up Helper)

### Strategic Fit

- Show direct contribution to net zero and business sustainability support in Milton Keynes
- Emphasise multi-business benefit and city-wide relevance

### Quality of Proposal

- Provide clear intervention logic: need -> activities -> outputs -> outcomes
- Include strong rationale for chosen delivery methods and cohorts

### Deliverability

- Demonstrate delivery team capability and readiness
- Include realistic timeline, milestones, dependencies, and risk management

### Outcomes

- Set specific, measurable, credible output and impact targets
- Explain assumptions behind estimated carbon/resource outcomes

### Value for Money

- Link all budget lines to delivery activities
- Demonstrate proportionate costs and meaningful reach/impact

## 9) Compliance Checklist Before Submission

- Project is revenue-only (no capital items)
- Activity benefits multiple businesses (not one)
- Activity supports Milton Keynes businesses within the authority area
- Costs are not double-funded
- Staff and overhead costs are clearly linked to project delivery
- Timeline and milestones are achievable within project period
- Outputs and outcomes are measurable and evidenced
