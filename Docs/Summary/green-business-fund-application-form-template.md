## Subsidy Control

Subsidy has the meaning given in the Subsidy Control Act (SCA) 2022.

When considering a grant award, the subsidy control regime under the SCA 2022 must be considered and relevant principles/requirements adhered to, where applicable.

Some subsidies may be exempt from many substantive requirements where awarded as Minimal Financial Assistance (MFA). MFA threshold is GBP 315,000 over the applicable period (current financial year to date plus previous two financial years).

Before awarding MFA subsidy, Milton Keynes City Council must issue an MFA notification and obtain written confirmation from the recipient that the threshold will not be exceeded.

The Council can only award subsidy as MFA after receiving this confirmation.

For more information, refer to the Subsidy Control Act 2022 guidance.

## Appendix 2: Activity Explicitly Excluded from Funding

The following are not eligible:

- Paid lobbying (external or in-house) intended to influence Parliament, Government, political activity, legislative or regulatory action
- Using grant funds to enable one part of government to challenge another on unrelated topics
- Using grant funding to petition for additional funding
- Entertaining expenses aimed at exerting undue influence to change government policy
- VAT reclaimable from HMRC (only irrecoverable VAT is eligible)
- Activities of a party political or exclusively religious nature
- Interest payments or service charge payments for finance leases
- Gifts or donations
- Statutory fines, criminal fines, or penalties
- Works/activities that any party already has a statutory duty to undertake, or that are fully funded by other sources
- Bad debts to related parties
- Payments for unfair dismissal or other compensation
- Depreciation or amortisation costs
- Contingencies and contingent liabilities
- Dividends
- Costs resulting from deferred payments to creditors
- Costs involved in winding up a company
- Legal expenses in respect of litigation
- Costs incurred by individuals setting up/contributing to private pension schemes
- Payments that breach or are contrary to the funding agreement or UK legislation
