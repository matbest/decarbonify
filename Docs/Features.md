# Decarbonify Features and Capabilities

## What this tool is for

Many institutions, councils, and companies hold complex portfolios of assets that could be improved from a carbon and resource-efficiency perspective. The challenge is usually not intent, it is prioritisation: where to start, what will have the biggest impact, what is realistically deliverable, and what gives the best value for effort and budget.

Decarbonify helps teams that manage asset-heavy organisations identify where to focus first, compare opportunities across a portfolio, and turn carbon data into practical action plans.

The approach aligns with the Greenhouse Gas Protocol framing of emissions, including Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 classification, so organisations can structure analysis and reporting in a recognised standard.

Scope 3 can be the hardest area to measure in practice because data is often distributed, incomplete, or outside direct operational control. Decarbonify uses AI-assisted analysis to take a whole-portfolio view and surface opportunities in a ranked, easy-to-read format, helping teams identify where action is likely to deliver the best impact for effort.

## Core capabilities

- Portfolio modelling of assets as a hierarchy
- Support for nested structures such as site, building, room, equipment, land, and vehicles
- Asset-level and subtree-level emissions tracking
- Greenhouse Gas Protocol-aligned classification context for Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 emissions
- Manual and derived data fields with clear override logic
- Asset type templates with reusable input and output definitions
- Formula-based calculations for template outputs using a restricted arithmetic evaluator
- Baseline and hotspot visibility to identify high-emission assets quickly
- Recommendation generation with estimated savings per action
- Explainable recommendations with assumptions and calculation text
- Recommendation status tracking such as done and ignored
- Portfolio and subtree rollups for potential versus actualised savings
- AI-assisted freeform intake from listing text or notes into structured portfolio edits
- Step-by-step apply flow for drafted intake operations
- Duplicate asset detection with user-controlled merge actions
- Interactive chat assistant for portfolio questions and prioritisation guidance
- AI fallback behavior when no API key is configured
- Ontology-aware asset validation and parent-child relationship rules
- Reordering and editing of the hierarchy in-app
- Optional Google OAuth login and access restrictions by domain or email
- Optional Google Drive persistence for user portfolio state

## Feature detail by workflow

### 1. Capture and structure

- Build or import a portfolio JSON
- Add assets at root, child, or sibling level
- Keep taxonomy consistent with asset type templates
- Capture attributes and operational context on each asset

### 2. Quantify emissions

- Store emissions in a standard field per asset
- Distinguish produced emissions from sequestration effects
- Support scope-aware interpretation of emissions data using Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 categories
- Use manual values where known and derived values where estimated
- Roll up totals through the hierarchy for fast comparison
- Use AI to generate a broad portfolio overview and rank opportunities into readable priorities, including harder-to-quantify Scope 3 areas

### 3. Generate improvement options

- Produce recommendations per asset via AI or heuristics
- Ground recommendation savings to current baseline values
- Show assumptions and simple equations for transparency
- Regenerate recommendations when context changes

### 4. Prioritise action

- Compare done versus possible savings
- Track recommendation progress at asset and subtree levels
- Surface likely high-value interventions early
- Use chat to explore what to upgrade first and why

### 5. Deliver and report

- Keep an auditable structure of assets, assumptions, and actions
- Export and persist portfolio state for ongoing delivery cycles
- Reuse templates and workflows across multiple organisations

## Why this is useful for asset-holding organisations

- Reduces analysis paralysis by turning complex portfolios into a structured decision space
- Helps teams focus on the interventions with the strongest likely impact per unit of effort
- Improves consistency of assessments across sites and business units
- Supports carbon planning, engagement, and delivery in one practical workflow
- Makes technical recommendations understandable to non-specialist stakeholders

## Typical users

- Local authorities and public bodies
- Facilities and estate management teams
- Multi-site businesses
- Sustainability consultants and delivery partners
- Community and voluntary organisations supporting business decarbonisation

## In short

Decarbonify is a practical portfolio decarbonisation workspace: it helps organisations move from scattered asset data and uncertainty to structured baselines, prioritised actions, and measurable progress.
